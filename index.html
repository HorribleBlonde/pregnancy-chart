Conception (≈ week 2) and due date (week 40)

How weeks are counted. Pregnancy weeks run from the first day of the last period, as doctors count them, so conception happens around week 2 and the due date is week 40.

About “trying”. Bars that start “when you start trying” assume about three months of trying before conception. If you enter a “start of trying” date above, read the chart against your own dates.

Guidance differs by country. Sources are mostly UK (NHS, NICE) and US (CDC, ACOG, USPSTF). Where they disagree on timing, the item says so. Your own midwife or doctor's advice comes first.

General information, not medical advice. It doesn't cover individual conditions, medicines or high-risk pregnancies, and recommendations change. Talk to your doctor or midwife before starting supplements or making changes.
